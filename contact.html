FAQ

Questions, answered.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be requested at booking, subject to availability.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes — most rates include free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. Check your confirmation email for the exact policy on your booking.

Do you charge for Wi-Fi or parking?

No. High-speed Wi-Fi and on-site parking are complimentary for all guests. EV charging is available for a small fee.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, in our pet-friendly rooms, for a one-time $35 cleaning fee. Let us know when you book so we can prepare a pet bed and bowls.

Is the AI concierge really available 24/7?

It is. Aura can book rooms, answer questions, and send human-support notifications at any hour — day or night.
